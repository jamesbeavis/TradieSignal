About this data. Council development records come from the NSW Planning Portal ePlanning open data APIs, © State of New South Wales (Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure), used under CC BY 4.0 and refreshed daily. Declared costs are lodged by applicants and are not audited by council; we cross-check each one against floor area, dwelling count and lot count and mark anything that fails. Opportunity scores, electrical estimates and contact timing are Tradiesignal's own analysis and are estimates, not quotes. Always verify a project against the council's DA tracker before committing time or money.
